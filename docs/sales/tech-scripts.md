# Hero Club: scripts for technicians

Short on purpose. Each one takes about 30 seconds to say. Say it once and
stop.

## 1. After a one-off job, at the walkthrough

The main one. The house looks its best and the customer is standing in front
of it.

> "Everything's done. Take a look at the gutters and the front windows
> before I pack up. Most people I do this for end up calling us back two or
> three times a year. We have a membership called the Hero Club that covers
> it: gutters, a house wash and windows, already scheduled, for one monthly
> price. For a home your size it starts at $54 a month. Want me to show you
> the plans? It takes two minutes on my phone."

If yes, open the signup page and hand them the phone. If no:

> "No problem. I'll leave this card in case you want to look later."

$54 is the Bronze price for a home up to 2,500 sq ft. For a home of 2,501 to
3,500 sq ft, say $71. For a larger home, say "the office will give you your
price."

## 2. On arrival, to plant the idea

Do not pitch here.

> "Morning, I'm [NAME] with Window Hero, here for your [SERVICE]. I'll be
> about [TIME]. When I'm done I'll walk you around, and I'll mention how our
> members get this done without having to call."

## 3. When the customer asks "how often should I do this?"

The best opening a tech gets.

> "Gutters, at least twice a year around here with these trees. House wash
> once a year. That's exactly what our Bronze plan is: two gutter cleanings,
> a house wash and an exterior window cleaning, spread into one monthly
> payment. We schedule it so you don't have to remember."

## 4. Knocking on the neighbors' doors after a job

> "Hi, I'm [NAME] with Window Hero. We just finished at [NEIGHBOR'S FIRST
> NAME]'s house next door, so you may have seen the truck. I'm not here to
> sell you anything today. We're on this street regularly, and I wanted to
> leave this in case you'd like the same done. The code takes you to the
> plans and prices."

## 5. To an existing member, at the end of a visit

> "All set, and your next visit is [SEASON]. One thing: if a neighbor joins
> the Hero Club and gives us your name and address, you get a credit on your
> bill worth their first month. Anyone on the street been asking about your
> windows?"

## Answers to what customers push back with

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "What if I want to cancel?" | "You can cancel in the first three business days for a full refund. After that it's a 12-month plan with 30 days' notice, and you only settle up for work we've already done." |
| "What if it rains?" | "We move the visit. You never lose one." |
| "What if I'm not happy with a visit?" | "Tell us within seven days and we come back and fix it free." |
| "I need to ask my spouse." | "Of course. The plans and prices are all on this card, and nothing's charged until you sign." |
| "That's a lot per month." | "It's the same work you'd call us for anyway, spread over the year so there's no big bill. You also get 10% off anything extra." |
| "Do I pay today?" | "No. You send a request, our office calls to confirm, and then you sign and pay the down payment." |

The discount on extras is 10% on Bronze, 15% on Silver and 20% on Gold.

## Three rules

- **Ask once.** A customer who says no today often signs up from the card
  later. Pushing costs us the review.
- **Never quote a price for a home over 3,500 sq ft.** Say "the office will
  give you your price."
- **Log it.** Tell the office who you spoke to, so the callback can say "you
  spoke with [TECH] yesterday."
