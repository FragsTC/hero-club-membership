# Referral messages

Copy, fill in the square brackets, and send. There are no links or codes:
the neighbor tells Window Hero the member's name and street address when
they join.

The email versions of these are ready-made ServiceMinder templates in
`email-templates/` (numbers 9 to 12 in `HERO-CLUB-EMAILS.md`). The wording
below is for texts, or for email if you are not using the templates.

Have the referral terms to hand for anyone who asks. They are in
`referral-terms.md`.

## Telling a member about it

Send it when a member becomes Active, and again after their first visit.

> Hi [FIRST NAME], thank you for being a Hero Club member. If a neighbor
> would like their home looked after too, send them our way. When they join
> and give us your name and street address, you get a credit on your bill
> equal to their first monthly charge. I've attached how it works.

## The ask, after a visit

Send it the day after a visit, with the visit's photos if you have them.
This is when a member is happiest with you.

> Hi [FIRST NAME], your [SERVICE] is done and the photos are attached. If a
> neighbor asks who did it, have them give us your name and address when
> they join the Hero Club. You get a credit on your bill equal to their
> first monthly charge.

## To the referrer, when their neighbor names them

> Hi [FIRST NAME], [NEIGHBOR'S FIRST NAME] is joining the Hero Club and
> told us you referred them. Thank you. Once they are signed up and their
> first monthly payment goes through, your credit goes on your bill.

## To the referrer, when the referral counts

> Hi [FIRST NAME], [NEIGHBOR'S FIRST NAME] is now a Hero Club member, so
> you have a $[CREDIT] credit, equal to their first monthly charge. It comes
> off your next Hero Club payment[, and the rest off the payments after
> that]. Thank you for spreading the word. You can refer up to 12 neighbors
> a year.

## When a referral does not count

> Hi [FIRST NAME], thank you for sending [NEIGHBOR'S FIRST NAME] our way.
> This one does not qualify for the referral credit because [REASON: they
> have been a Hero Club member before / they did not complete their signup].
> You are welcome to refer anyone new.

## Card wording

Front:

> **REFER A NEIGHBOR, GET A MONTH**
> Your neighbor is a Hero Club member.
> When you join, tell us who sent you.
>
> Referred by: ______________________
> Their address: ____________________

The member writes their own name and address on the card before handing it
over.

Back, small print:

> The referring member's household receives a bill credit equal to the new
> member's first monthly charge, once the new member has paid their down
> payment and first monthly charge. Tell us who referred you before you
> sign. Ask us for the full terms.
