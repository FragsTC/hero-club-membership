# Referral messages

Copy, fill in the square brackets, and send. Keep the link to the terms in
anything that says "a month free".

Terms link: `https://heroclub-membership.netlify.app/referral-terms.html`

## The member's link

The link maker writes this one for you, with the member's own link in it.
Send it when a member becomes Active.

## The ask, after a visit

Send it the day after a visit, with the visit's photos if you have them.
This is when a member is happiest with you.

> Hi [FIRST NAME], your [SERVICE] is done and the photos are attached. If a
> neighbor asks who did it, send them your link and you each get a month
> free: [MEMBER'S LINK]

## To the referrer, when their neighbor signs up

> Hi [FIRST NAME], [NEIGHBOR'S FIRST NAME] just asked to join the Hero Club
> through your link. Thank you. Once they are signed up and their first
> monthly payment goes through, your credit goes on your bill.

## To the referrer, when the referral counts

> Hi [FIRST NAME], [NEIGHBOR'S FIRST NAME] is now a Hero Club member, so
> $[CREDIT] is coming off your next Hero Club payment. Thank you for
> spreading the word. Your link works as many times as you like, up to 12 a
> year: [MEMBER'S LINK]

## To the new member, when the referral counts

> Hi [FIRST NAME], welcome to the Hero Club. Because [REFERRER'S FIRST NAME]
> referred you, your twelfth monthly payment is on us. You can refer your own
> neighbors too, and you each get a month free: [NEW MEMBER'S LINK]

## When a referral does not count

> Hi [FIRST NAME], thank you for sending [NEIGHBOR'S FIRST NAME] our way.
> This one does not qualify for the referral credit because [REASON: they
> have been a Hero Club member before / they did not complete their signup].
> Your link still works for anyone new.

## Card wording

Front:

> **GIVE A MONTH, GET A MONTH**
> Your neighbor [MEMBER'S FIRST NAME] is a Hero Club member.
> Join through this code and you each get a month free.

Back, small print:

> The new member's twelfth month is free. The referring member receives a
> bill credit worth the new member's monthly charge, up to one month of
> their own plan, once the new member has paid their down payment and first
> monthly charge. Full terms: heroclub-membership.netlify.app/referral-terms.html

Print the member's QR code from the link maker next to the front text.
