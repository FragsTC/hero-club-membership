# Notes for legal review

**This agreement is a draft. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.** Have a
North Carolina lawyer read `hero-club-membership-agreement.md` before any
customer signs it. This page lists what to ask them and what still needs
filling in.

## What to ask the lawyer to check

1. **Automatic renewal (N.C. Gen. Stat. 75-41).** Is the "Renewal" block
   clear and conspicuous enough where it sits, directly above the
   acceptance line? Is a notice emailed 30 days before renewal sufficient?
   The statute as we read it requires written notice at least 15 and no more
   than 45 days before renewal, and makes the renewal clause void if the
   rules are not followed. A 2025 bill, HB 188, proposed changing the notice
   window and adding display and online-cancellation requirements. We did
   not confirm whether it passed. Ask what the law is today.
2. **The 3-business-day cancellation.** Memberships will sometimes be sold
   by a technician at the customer's home. Do the home-solicitation sale
   rules (federal Cooling-Off Rule and N.C. Gen. Stat. 25A-38 to 25A-42)
   apply, and if so do we need the prescribed "Notice of Cancellation" form
   and wording, in addition to this section? What if the customer asks for a
   visit inside the 3 days and then cancels?
3. **The cancellation settlement.** A member who leaves early pays the
   regular one-time price of services received, less what they have paid,
   capped at the monthly charges left in the year. Could this be read as a
   penalty? Is the cap enough? Should the regular prices be fixed in
   Schedule A at signing, as drafted, or may they change?
4. **Charging the card on file.** The agreement authorizes charges for the
   deposit, monthly charges, add-ons, renewal-year charges and a
   cancellation balance. Is that authorization sufficient for recurring card
   charges and for a one-off cancellation balance, or should the balance be
   invoiced first?
5. **The renewal year.** A renewal year has no deposit and is 12 monthly
   charges at the price in force at renewal. Is "our price for your plan and
   home size at that time", stated in the notice, a sufficient disclosure of
   a term that changes on renewal?
6. **Sale of the home.** The membership passes to a buyer who agrees in
   writing, otherwise it ends and the settlement applies. Any problem with
   either branch?
7. **Window Hero ending a membership.** We may end it with 30 days' notice
   and refund any amount paid beyond the value received. Is that adequate?
8. **What is missing.** This draft has no limitation of liability, damage
   or property-condition terms. Does Window Hero's existing service terms
   in ServiceMinder cover those, and should this agreement refer to them?
9. **Electronic signature.** Acceptance and signature happen on a
   ServiceMinder proposal. Is the acceptance line at the end sufficient?

## Things this draft decides that the owner should know

- Add-ons and extra services are billed on their own, not in the monthly
  charge.
- Unused services do not carry over to a renewal year.
- If Window Hero ends a membership for a reason other than a failed payment,
  the member owes nothing more and is refunded any amount paid beyond the
  value received.
- The customer must provide outdoor water and access.
