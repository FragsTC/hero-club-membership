ask for one, we bill it on its own after the work is done, at your member

## Scheduling your visits

We schedule each visit within the season it belongs to and confirm the date
with you ahead of time. If weather, safety or access to your home gets in the
way, we move the visit to a new date. You do not lose it.

To do the work we need access to the outside of your home, a working outdoor
water tap, and for interior window cleaning, someone to let us in.

## Our guarantee

If you are not happy with any part of a visit, tell us within 7 days and we
will come back and fix it at no charge.

## Changing your mind in the first 3 business days

You may cancel for any reason within 3 business days of signing, and we will
refund everything you have paid. Email [OFFICE EMAIL] or call [OFFICE PHONE].
We do not carry out any visit during those 3 business days.

## Cancelling

After the first 3 business days, you may cancel at any time by giving us 30
days' notice by email or phone. Your membership ends 30 days after we receive
your notice. Monthly charges that fall due before that end date are still
charged, and visits already scheduled before it still take place.

Membership spreads the cost of your services evenly over the year, so early
in the year you may have received more service than you have paid for. When
a membership ends early, we settle up like this:

1. We add up the services you received, at our regular one-time prices
   (Schedule A below).
2. We subtract everything you have paid in down payment and monthly charges.
3. If you received more than you paid for, you owe the difference. It is
   never more than the monthly charges that were left in your 12 months.
4. If you paid for more than you received, you owe nothing more and no refund
   is due.

Any balance is charged to your card on file when the membership ends. If
that charge does not go through, we send you an invoice for it.

Window Hero may also end your membership with 30 days' notice. If we do that
for any reason other than a failed payment, you owe nothing more, and if you
have paid for more than you received we refund the difference.

## If a payment fails

If a charge to your card fails, we will let you know and you have 10 days to
update your card. We hold your visits until the payment goes through. If the
payment is still unpaid after 30 days, your membership ends on that day. There
is no further notice period, and we settle up as described in steps 1 to 4
of "Cancelling" above.

## If you move

If you sell your home, your membership can pass to the buyer for the rest of
the 12 months if the buyer agrees in writing to take it over. Otherwise it
ends on the day you tell us you have moved. There is no notice period, and we
settle up as described in steps 1 to 4 of "Cancelling" above.
