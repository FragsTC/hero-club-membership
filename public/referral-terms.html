Hero Club Give a month, get a month

Refer a neighbor to the Hero Club. When they join, you each get a month free.

What each of you gets

  • The new member gets one month of their own plan free. It comes off their twelfth monthly charge.
  • The member who referred them gets a credit on their bill equal to the new member's monthly charge, up to one month of their own plan. It comes off their next monthly charge.

When it counts

  • The new member signs their Hero Club agreement and pays their down payment and their first monthly charge.
  • The member who referred them is still an active Hero Club member on that day.
  • The new member's request names the member who referred them, through that member's link or by typing their name when signing up.

The rules

  • Only active Hero Club members can refer.
  • The new member must be a household that has never had a Hero Club membership. Past one-time Window Hero customers count as new. Your own address does not.
  • You can earn up to 12 referral credits in each membership year.
  • A credit you cannot use this year carries into your renewal year. Credits are lost if your membership ends.
  • A new member who leaves before their twelfth monthly charge does not receive their free month.
  • If two members refer the same neighbor, the name on the neighbor's signup request decides it.
  • Credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred.
  • Window Hero can change or end this offer at any time. Credits already earned are honored.

Your Hero Club Membership Agreement covers everything else about your membership.
